### M1. Brief vs pack: one file-inventory disagreement

The project brief at the top of this session lists the five source files as `subscribers.csv, viewing_activity.csv, cancellations.xlsx, pricing_history.csv, viewer_survey.pdf`. The packs use `sw_subscribers.csv, sw_viewing_activity.xlsx, sw_cancellations.xlsx, sw_content_catalogue.csv, sw_viewer_survey.pdf`. There is no `pricing_history.csv` anywhere in the 10 chapters.

The pack is **internally consistent** — the discrepancy is between the brief and the pack, not within the pack. However, the Day 0 narrative on line 522 implies "senior team thinks the cause is price," which sets up an expectation that pricing data exists somewhere. The pack never gives the learner a way to disprove the price hypothesis using actual pricing data — `sw_content_catalogue.csv` is about content metadata, not pricing.

**Recommended fix:** Either (a) update the project brief to match the pack's file list, or (b) add a small `pricing_history.csv` and a one-page "price hypothesis ruled out" callout in Day 4 or Day 5. Option (a) is cheaper and probably correct — the price hypothesis is named only as a misdirection in the opener.

## Minor findings — polish

### MIN1. Day 4 SUMIFS upper-bound edge case

**File:** `Day04_Reading_the_Data.html` line 836
**Current:**
```excel
=SUMIFS(tblActivity[duration], tblActivity[date], ">="&DATE(2025,4,1), tblActivity[date], "<="&DATE(2026,3,31)) / 60
```
**Issue:** If `tblActivity[date]` contains any non-zero time component, `<=DATE(2026,3,31)` resolves to `<=2026-03-31 00:00:00` and silently drops sessions logged later on 31/03/2026. Real data sometimes carries timestamps.
**Recommended fix:**
```excel
=SUMIFS(tblActivity[duration], tblActivity[date], ">="&DATE(2025,4,1), tblActivity[date], "<"&DATE(2026,4,1)) / 60
```
Or add a note that the formula assumes date-only values.

## Audit verdict

The programme is publishable as-is for a soft launch. The two critical findings are surface-level cross-reference fixes, not content errors. The moderate findings are coverage gaps (Day 0 / Day 1 missing one callout type each) and one narrative thread that's named "formally" on Day 9 without having been named earlier. The minor findings are polish.

**Nothing in this audit changes the structural integrity of the programme.** The numbers add up, the characters are consistent, the named devices propagate through Day 0 → home chapter → Day 9 Week in Review, and the red-flag warnings (Delete Anomaly, Three Questions Before You Delete, "I don't know" reframes, bluff warning) are present and worded consistently.

The earlier user concern — *"there is nothing more anxiety-causing than contradictory training — it needs to be smooth and narratively and tonally consistent"* — is well-met by the current state of the packs.
